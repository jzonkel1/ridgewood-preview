Good to know

Questions, answered

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. We'll give you pricing before any repairs are made, so nothing gets fixed until you've said yes.

Do you charge more on weekends?

No. The price is the same on Sunday as it is on Tuesday. We're open Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm, Saturday 9am to 6pm, and Sunday 9am to 4pm.

How do I book?

Your choice. Call 361-608-9553 and a real person will get you scheduled, or book online and pick a time that works for you.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Mostly we work on homes, and we also take light commercial work like offices and shopping strip centers. We don't take restaurant, medical or industrial jobs.

Do you work on window units?

No, we don't service window units. If you're ready to move past one, ask us about a ductless mini-split.

What brands do you install?

Mostly Rheem, plus Daikin and Goodman. We'll walk you through the options that fit your home.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, financing is available. Ask about it when you call or when we go over your options.
