Good to know

Club questions

How much is the Club Membership?

Ask for membership pricing when you call or when we reach out after you sign up. Extra systems are added to the same membership.

When are the checkups?

Twice a year: in the spring before the cooling season and in the fall before heating season. We'll remind you when it's time.

What if my system breaks between checkups?

Members get priority service, no service fee and 10% off the repair.

Do I need a new system to join?

No. The club is for any working system we can service. If we find a problem on the first checkup, you'll get the price before anything is fixed.
