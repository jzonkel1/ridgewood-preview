Meet Thomas Martinez

Thomas "Tommy" Martinez founded Ridgewood Air Conditioning & Heating in Ingleside and still runs the jobs himself. Customers describe him as "an ace HVAC mechanic" and "high tech with a high bar for integrity," and they mention the same things over and over: he explains what's wrong, he shows you, and his price is fair.

That's on purpose. Ridgewood checks systems with thermal imaging cameras and live digital testing, and every checkup and repair comes with readings and photos. You see what we see.

And the business runs on three simple promises:

"A real person answers the phone, the price is the same on Sunday as it is on Tuesday, and we'll give you pricing before any repairs are made."Thomas Martinez, owner

When a customer's heat went out during a cold snap and the part had to be ordered, Thomas brought them a room heater to use until it came in. That's the standard.
