Portland's summers are long.

Portland is one of our busiest towns, a short drive from the shop in Ingleside. It's a town of family homes, many of them built in the last few decades, and a lot of those homes are now on their first or second AC system.

That's the age when the questions start: is this repair worth it, or is it time to replace? We'll test the system, show you the readings and give you both numbers, so you can decide with real information instead of a sales pitch.

Portland's two-story homes have their own problem: the upstairs that never cools down. That's usually airflow, ductwork or system sizing, and it's fixable.

Good to know

Portland questions

Why is my upstairs so much hotter than downstairs?

Heat rises, attics over second floors get extremely hot, and upstairs ducts are often long or undersized. Balancing the airflow, fixing the ducts or adding a zone or mini-split usually solves it.

Do you come to Portland on weekends?

Yes. We're open Saturday and Sunday, and the price is the same on Sunday as on Tuesday.

How old is too old for an AC system?

There's no single number, but on the coast many systems start costing more in repairs than they're worth somewhere past the ten-year mark. We'll give you the numbers for yours.

Can I book online?

Yes. Pick a time on our booking page, or call and a real person will get you scheduled.
